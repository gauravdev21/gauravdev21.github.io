Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how JK Electricals Dashboard ("the App") handles your information.

1. What the App Does

JK Electricals Dashboard is a private business dashboard that allows authorised users to view business data (sales, purchases, debtors, creditors) exported from Tally accounting software and stored in Google Drive.

2. Information We Access

The App requests read-only access to your Google Drive solely to retrieve Excel files from a specific designated folder. The App does not access any other files, folders, emails, contacts, or personal data in your Google account.

3. How We Use Your Information

The App uses your Google account only to authenticate you as an authorised user and to read the designated business data files from Google Drive. Your data is displayed within the App on your device only. We do not transmit, store, or share your data with any third parties.

4. Data Storage

Business data is stored locally on your device (browser local storage) for convenience so you do not need to reload it every session. This data never leaves your device.

5. Who Has Access

This is a private app for internal business use only. Access is restricted to explicitly authorised users. No public access is provided.

6. Third Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services:

— Google Sign-In and Google Drive API for authentication and file access. Google's privacy policy applies: policies.google.com/privacy

— GitHub Pages for hosting the App. GitHub's privacy policy applies: GitHub Privacy Statement

7. Data Security

Access to the App is protected by Google OAuth authentication. Only accounts explicitly added as authorised users can sign in. All data transmission uses HTTPS encryption.

8. Children's Privacy

This App is not intended for use by children under 13 years of age.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

10. Contact
